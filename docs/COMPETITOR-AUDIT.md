## Audit method

For each selected product, record the product name, URL, access date, tested device/browser, user task, observed behavior, and evidence (screenshots or reproducible steps).

## Comparison areas

- Lab discovery: subject taxonomy, search, filters, and experiment detail hierarchy.
- Learning loop: theory, Theory Test, simulation, Final Test, and report support.
- Simulation quality: model transparency, input validation, units, output explanation, and repeatability.
- Accessibility: keyboard access, semantics, contrast, captions/text alternatives, and reduced motion.
- Student continuity: account requirements, privacy, progress retention, and export options.

## Finding labels

- **Verified observation:** directly reproduced and supported by dated evidence.
- **Hypothesis:** a question to investigate; not stated as a defect.
- **Opportunity:** a user need that may be worth supporting, without asserting a competitor failure.
