# Competitor audit framework

Virtual Labs' IIT Roorkee Digital Electronics Lab-I, Experiment 01 was reviewed on 2026-10-22 to align the experiment section sequence. This was a focused reference review, not a broad competitor audit.

## Verified reference observation

- The experiment presents separate Aim, Theory, Pretest, Procedure, Simulation, Posttest, References, and Feedback sections that can be selected from navigation.
- Source: [Virtual Labs, IIT Roorkee — truth tables for logic gates](https://de-iitr.vlabs.ac.in/exp/truth-table-gates/).
- TattvaLab keeps its Medi-Caps-inspired palette and sample-content notice, and adds a printable Report after Feedback.

## Audit method

For each selected product, record the product name, URL, access date, tested device/browser, user task, observed behavior, and evidence (screenshots or reproducible steps).

## Comparison areas

- Lab discovery: subject taxonomy, search, filters, and experiment detail hierarchy.
- Learning loop: theory, pre-test, simulation, post-test, and report support.
- Simulation quality: model transparency, input validation, units, output explanation, and repeatability.
- Accessibility: keyboard access, semantics, contrast, captions/text alternatives, and reduced motion.
- Student continuity: account requirements, privacy, progress retention, and export options.

## Finding labels

- **Verified observation:** directly reproduced and supported by dated evidence.
- **Hypothesis:** a question to investigate; not stated as a defect.
- **Opportunity:** a user need that may be worth supporting, without asserting a competitor failure.

Populate this audit after selecting products and completing reproducible task-based reviews.
